LangTechDepot installer
=======================

Double-click:  setup-langtechdepot.bat

You can do that right here, without unzipping anything. Leave this zip window
open until the installer has finished. It will ask you for your token - paste
it in and press Enter.

If you have not got a token yet, or something goes wrong, the step-by-step
instructions with pictures are here:

    https://sillsdev.github.io/langtechdepot/

Only if the installer says it could not get Syncthing: unzip this download
first (right-click the zip -> Extract All -> Extract), put syncthing.exe from
https://syncthing.net/downloads/ beside setup-langtechdepot.bat in the folder
that opens, and double-click the .bat there.
