LangTechDepot installer
=======================

Double-click:  run-setup-langtechdepot.bat

That is all. It will ask you for your token - paste it in and press Enter.

If you have not got a token yet, or something goes wrong, the step-by-step
instructions with pictures are here:

    https://sillsdev.github.io/langtechdepot/

Both files in this folder are needed, and they must stay side by side. If you
are reading this inside the zip window, unpack the folder first: right-click
the zip -> Extract All -> Extract.
