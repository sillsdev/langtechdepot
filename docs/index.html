SIL language-technology software and training material, copied to your machine and kept up to date by itself. About ten minutes. You do not need administrator rights, and you can stop after any step and come back later.

What happens

  1. Step 1Get your token
  2. Step 2Download
  3. Step 3Run it
  4. Step 4Pick your folders

Step 1 happens on our server, which emails you a token — a password that works once, on one machine. The other three happen on your computer. Choose your system above and it walks you through all four.

Already been through this once? Setting up a second machine just means starting again from step 1: each machine needs its own token. Windows · Linux
Something not working? The help page covers the handful of things that normally go wrong, and how to reach a person.

LangTechDepot distributes the SIL language technology software repository. It is built on Syncthing, which is free and open source. Your machine only ever receives files — nothing on it is sent anywhere.
