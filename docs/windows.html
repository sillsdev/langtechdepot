LangTechDepot

Windows: four steps

Do them in order. Nothing here needs administrator rights, and nothing is left half-installed if you stop partway — you can pick up again at any point.

  1. Step 1Get your token
  2. Step 2Download
  3. Step 3Run it
  4. Step 4Pick your folders
1

Get your token

Fill in the short form on our server. We check each request by hand, then email you a token — a long password that works once, on this one computer.

Get my token Opens depot.langtech.cloud. While you wait for the email, carry on with step 2: the installer asks for the token when it needs it.
2

Download the installer

One small file. Your Downloads folder is a fine place for it.

Download for Windows langtechdepot-windows.zip · version dev · about 20 KB
No need to unzip it. Double-click the downloaded file and a window opens showing two files: the installer, setup-langtechdepot.bat, and START-HERE.txt, a short note.
3

Double-click setup-langtechdepot.bat

Do it right there in the zip window, and leave that window open until the installer has finished. A black window opens and does the work. When it asks for your token, paste it in — right-click inside the window, or press Ctrl+V — and press Enter.

If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway. That warning appears for anything downloaded from the internet that has not been through Microsoft’s paid signing programme.
If it says it could not get Syncthing: this machine has no winget, or winget could not reach the internet. Download Syncthing for Windows from syncthing.net/downloads (the 64-bit archive) and take syncthing.exe out of it. Then unzip the LangTechDepot download — right-click it → Extract All → Extract — put syncthing.exe in the folder that opens, beside setup-langtechdepot.bat, and double-click the .bat there.
4

Pick the folders you want

First the installer asks where to keep your LangTechDepot Assets — the folders of files that come from the depot. Click OK for the suggested place, C:\Users\you\LangTechDepot\Assets, or pick another drive, such as an external disk or a USB disk you carry.

Then, within a minute or two, a window lists every folder on offer, with its size and a line saying what is in it. Tick the ones you want and click Apply. With the keyboard: ↓ and ↑ move through the list, Space ticks or unticks, and Enter applies.

Anything left unticked is set aside, and Syncthing stops offering it. That is not permanent: double-click Change my Assets in your LangTechDepot folder (or run the installer again) and the list comes back, with Also display ignored folders to bring back anything you passed over.
Not sure which? Each folder is named for the part of the library it carries, and its description says who it is for. If you are not sure, scroll right down to the bottom of the list and tick Win_everything_en — Windows programs, everything, less some large folders. Mind the size column if your connection is slow.
Seeing only a few? The library is still being moved onto this system a folder at a time, so not all of it is on offer yet. You do not need to register again — when more have arrived, double-click Change my Assets and the new ones will be in the list.

That is the whole setup. Syncthing starts with your computer from now on and keeps those folders current by itself. The folders only ever receive — nothing on your machine is sent anywhere.

Afterwards, everything is in one place for everyone. Open File Explorer, and under Quick access double-click LangTechDepot. Inside it:

  • Assets — the folders of files coming from the depot. Double-click it to see them. (If you chose somewhere else for them, this is a shortcut that takes you there.)
  • Change my Assets — opens the list again, to add folders, take back ones you ignored, or stop ones you no longer need.
  • Am I up-to-date, and advanced management — opens the Syncthing page (usually localhost:8384) in your browser. When every folder there says Up to Date, you have everything. Check it before you travel.
  • READ-ME.txt — all of this, for later.
Setting up a second machine? Start again at step 1: each machine needs its own token.
Stuck? The help page covers what usually goes wrong. If the black window showed an error, copy that text — it is what lets us fix things quickly.
